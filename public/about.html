Who I am

I build technology with a reason behind it.

My work sits at the intersection of software development and practical problem solving. I enjoy taking an idea, understanding the people who will use it, designing a sensible technical foundation and turning it into a working product.

I work across frontend development, backend engineering, APIs, databases and security-focused engineering. I care about how a product behaves after launch as much as how it looks when it is first delivered.

I also enjoy building technology for student communities and organizations. These projects have given me experience thinking about real users, limited resources, administration, content management and long-term maintainability.

Today, Codex Inc is the professional technology brand through which I build and collaborate on digital products remotely.

How I work

Simple process. Serious engineering.

01 — Understand
Clarify the problem, users, goals and constraints before writing unnecessary scripts.
02 — Build
Choose a practical architecture and build the interface, backend, data layer and integrations.
03 — Improve
Test the important flows, address reliability and security concerns, then prepare the product for continued growth.
